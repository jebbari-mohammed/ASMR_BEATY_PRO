Subscription help

Use Restore Purchases in the app while signed in to the same account. Apple and Google manage billing, cancellation, and refunds through their stores. Deleting an app account does not cancel a store subscription.

Account and privacy

You can delete your account and saved app data from Settings. If you cannot sign in or deletion fails, email us from your account address so we can help. See the account deletion instructions, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service.
