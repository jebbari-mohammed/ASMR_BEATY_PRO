# Search evidence for the US English launch

Checked October 3, 2026. The primary market is **United States / English**, as
selected by the owner. This is a snapshot to guide truthful content and store
metadata, not a ranking forecast.

English, Google search sites only, and the default trailing twelve-month

`10040052-2627-0367-0000-a95a2678d972`.

The endpoint did not return volume for several product-opening-date and shelf
queries in the same request. Missing data is not zero demand. Google Ads search
volume is approximate, may combine close variants, and is **web search demand**,
not App Store search volume. Its competition field describes paid ads, not
organic ranking difficulty. See the [DataForSEO endpoint
documentation](https://docs.dataforseo.com/v3/keywords_data/google_ads/search_volume/live/).

One live DataForSEO Google organic SERP request for `skincare routine order`
used United States, English, iOS mobile, and depth 10. Task
`10040052-2627-0121-0000-a7b0d436d8c7` returned status `20000`. The
visible organic leaders included Origins, Cosmopolitan, the American Academy
of Dermatology, The Ordinary, skincare.com, and CeraVe. A small new site
cannot assume a top position for this broad query. The opportunity is to make
the existing guide genuinely useful and source-backed while the homepage
clearly explains the app's distinct, working routine flow.

## App Store search snapshot

One DataForSEO Apple App Searches task for `skincare routine` used United
States / English and depth 100. Task
`10040053-2627-0428-0000-c602fcb9f016` completed with status `20000`.
The top five titles were BasicBeauty, Skincare Routine Planner, Charm,
FeelinMySkin, and Skincare Routine. This is a competitive category snapshot,
not an estimate of app keyword volume or evidence that a title change would
improve rank. ASMR Beauty Pro is still in private testing, so its absence from
public results says nothing about its eventual rank. Preserve the distinctive
brand name; use the subtitle, accurate description, and real screenshots to
explain the product. [Apple says search considers text relevance and user
behavior](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/), including downloads,
ratings, and reviews. Public product page optimization requires a Ready for
Distribution app; useful evaluation also needs traffic, so it is not a
private-test shortcut.

Google Search Console has indexed the homepage and routine guide, but the
sitemap report still shows a temporary processing error. Fresh crawls were

and indexed content after Search Console processes new data. Once the mobile

evidence. Never claim a rank or conversion lift from search-volume estimates.
