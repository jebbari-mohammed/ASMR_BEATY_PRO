Privacy policy

This policy explains the information used by the current ASMR Beauty Pro routine app.

Effective October 3, 2026

On this page

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email jabbarimed2020@gmail.com.

Information we use

  • Account: your email address and Firebase account identifier for sign-in and account recovery.
  • Your starting answers: goals, skin feel, sensitivity, available time, routine experience, sunscreen habit, and motivation are saved to your account to shape your starting plan.
  • Your activity: routine steps you create, dates and steps you check off, optional daily skin-feel choices you make, and product names, brands, categories, and opening dates you add to your shelf. Each daily skin-feel choice is a fixed option without a note or photo.
  • Free access and membership: if you choose the one-time 10-day free access, its start and end dates are saved to your account. If you subscribe, store purchase identifiers and subscription status are supplied through RevenueCat, Apple, or Google. We do not receive your full payment card number.
  • Device: technical data needed for security and App Check verification. Optional reminder preferences and onboarding completion are stored on your device.

Photos and AI

The current release does not ask for or upload face photos, perform skin analysis, or run an AI coach. If these features are introduced, we will explain the data use and choices before enabling them.

Why we use it

We use this information to operate your account, show your routine and progress record, summarize any skin-feel choices you make over 14 days, sync your shelf, verify app access, prevent misuse, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations. We keep the one-time free-access record while your account is active to prevent starting the same offer again. The summary does not score or diagnose your skin. We use purchase records to understand subscription performance.

Service providers

Firebase provides account, database, cloud function, and security services. RevenueCat verifies membership and provides purchase and subscription analytics. Apple or Google processes in-app purchases. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Optional retailer links

If you choose a shopping link, an external retailer opens in your browser. We do not add your account ID, routine answers, or shelf entries to the link. The retailer, and any affiliate network disclosed beside a commissioned link, may process your visit or purchase under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details from the retailer.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and saved routine information while your account is active. Daily skin-feel choices receive a server-set 30-day expiration; Firestore deletes expired records asynchronously, so removal may not occur at the exact expiry time. You can delete the account and cloud data, including all check-ins and the free-access record, in the app’s Settings. If deletion fails, contact support so we can resolve it. Deleting the app account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel it with the store separately. Providers may retain records required for billing, security, or law under their policies.

Your choices

You can edit or remove routine steps and shelf entries, change or remove today’s skin-feel choice, turn off reminders, and request access to or correction of your information by email. You can delete your account in Settings. Local notification permission can be changed in device settings.

Security and location

We use authentication, per-account database rules, verified subscription access, and device attestation to protect the service. No system is perfectly secure. Firebase, RevenueCat, and the app stores may process data in countries different from yours.

Children and changes

The app is for adults aged 18 or older. We may update this policy as the product changes and will post the revised date here. Material changes will be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel.
