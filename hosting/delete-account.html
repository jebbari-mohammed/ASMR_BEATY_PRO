Account & data

Delete your account

You can permanently remove your ASMR Beauty Pro account and its saved app data at any time.

On this page

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to ASMR Beauty Pro.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and cloud data, confirm the action, and enter your account password when prompted.

The app deletes your account records before signing you out. If deletion does not finish, sign out, sign back in, and try again.

Request help without signing in

If you cannot access the app or deletion fails, email jabbarimed2020@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Include “ASMR Beauty Pro account deletion” in the subject. We will verify ownership before removing the account.

Delete some data and keep your account

To remove a routine step, open your morning or evening Routine, edit the step, and tap Remove this step. Past daily records remain. To remove a product from your shelf, open Shelf and tap the remove icon beside that product. For help deleting a particular daily record or other saved app data while keeping your account, email jabbarimed2020@gmail.com from your account address and describe the records. We will verify ownership first. Other routine and shelf records remain unless you remove them or request their deletion.

What is deleted

The in-app action removes your Firebase account and developer-controlled cloud records: your routine steps, daily completion history, product shelf, usage and entitlement cache, and any app files stored under your account. We do not keep a separate copy of those app records after successful deletion.

What may remain

Deleting your app account does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the store separately. Apple, Google, RevenueCat, and Firebase may retain transaction, fraud-prevention, security, or legal records under their own retention policies. We cannot delete records those providers must keep. If you contacted support by email, that correspondence may remain as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations.

See our Privacy Policy or email jabbarimed2020@gmail.com.
